Common questions

A few things people usually want to know before reaching out.

Do you work with my type of business? +
Yes — we work with most South African SMEs: retail, services, hospitality, professional services, ministries, and more. If you're not sure whether we're a fit, just ask us.
Can I pay monthly instead of one big upfront cost? +
Yes, all our website packages are billed monthly, so there's no large lump sum to find upfront.
Do I own my website? +
Yes — the site and its content are yours. Your monthly fee covers hosting, updates and support while you're an active client.
What if I already have a website? +
We can redesign or rebuild it, and help move your existing content, images and domain across smoothly.
Can you help with my logo or branding too? +
Basic branding touches are included with your website. Larger branding projects — like a full logo design — are quoted separately.
